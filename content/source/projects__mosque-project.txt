The mosque unit was constructed using modular container structures, finished in a clean white exterior to complement the surrounding greenery. Wide windows and a central entrance enhance natural light and ventilation, while the durable structure ensures long-term utility in outdoor conditions.
The interior of the mosque is designed with a spacious, serene layout ideal for prayer and gatherings. It features tiled flooring, soft lighting, proper ventilation, and minimalistic decor to maintain a peaceful environment. The space is functional, well-lit, and ready for community use.

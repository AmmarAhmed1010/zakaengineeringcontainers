This office container unit features a secure entrance door, insulated walls, and sliding windows to ensure ventilation and natural light. The exterior showcases a durable paint finish, while the interior layout is designed to provide a compact yet functional workspace.
The interior is fully equipped with essential office and utility features, including clean flooring, a storage cabinet section, and a compact washroom with a toilet and sink — offering both functionality and convenience for everyday use.

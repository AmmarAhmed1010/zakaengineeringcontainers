These initial images showcase the robust exterior of the call center, constructed using repurposed shipping containers—commonly transported via trucks for easy relocation. The container structure is designed for durability, mobility, and quick deployment. Its industrial aesthetic and steel-built frame offer strong resistance to environmental factors, making it an ideal solution for fast-paced commercial setups in diverse locations.
The upper-level exterior shows easy access via a durable steel staircase, designed for safety and long-term use. The rooftop area is finished with artificial turf and railing, creating a clean and comfortable outdoor seating space — perfect for guests to enjoy the scenic mountain views. These features enhance both the functionality and appeal of the hotel’s container structure.

Starting Date :

Ending Date :
25 March 2024
Location :

This portable office project was developed using a steel container, beginning with the exterior setup featuring a sleek finish and a sliding glass door. The next phase involved interior work, including electrical fittings, ceiling installation, and flooring. In the final stage, the office was fully installed on-site in G-6 Islamabad for the client, Chaudhary Zulfiqar, ready for functional use.
The interior of the portable office was designed for both functionality and comfort. It includes a clean partitioned workspace with flooring, a ceiling-mounted air conditioning system for effective climate control, and a full-size sliding glass door that allows natural light to enter while providing easy access.

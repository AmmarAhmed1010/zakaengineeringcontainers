The Noor Kitchen project utilizes bright, eye-catching yellow containers that reflect functionality with a modern aesthetic. The exterior design is simple yet purposeful, with clear branding that enhances visibility and identity. This modular setup allows for quick deployment and relocation, ideal for dynamic environments. The secure doors and clean finishes ensure both safety and weather resistance, making it suitable for long-term utility in various locations.
Inside the Noor Kitchen, the white, hygienic interior is thoughtfully arranged to ensure a clean and efficient workspace. Equipped with necessary utilities, including a sink, ventilation, lighting, and ample workspace, the design prioritizes functionality and safety. The modular structure is insulated and well-ventilated, creating a comfortable environment for staff. The layout maximizes the limited space while meeting the operational standards required for food preparation and handling.

Project Information
Client :
Chaudhary Zulfiqar
Starting Date :
16 Aug 2021
Ending Date :
20 Sep 2022
Location :
Near Naran
This custom-designed container project near Naran features a fully functional hotel and restaurant, built using high-quality shipping container units. The structure includes a branded front elevation, rooftop seating, and a spacious interior layout suitable for dining, kitchen, and lodging purposes. Designed to withstand the mountain climate, it offers both durability and visual appeal, making it an ideal stop for travelers on the scenic route. The project reflects Zaka Engineering’s commitment to smart, fast, and efficient modular construction.
The upper-level exterior shows easy access via a durable steel staircase, designed for safety and long-term use. The rooftop area is finished with artificial turf and railing, creating a clean and comfortable outdoor seating space — perfect for guests to enjoy the scenic mountain views. These features enhance both the functionality and appeal of the hotel’s container structure.
These interior photos highlight the cozy and inviting environment of the hotel’s dining and relaxation areas. The use of bright lighting, wooden paneling, and comfortable seating creates a warm ambiance for guests. The vibrant red and green theme adds a touch of charm, while the thoughtful layout ensures efficient use of space. These interior designs combine functionality with visual appeal, offering visitors a comfortable and memorable experience inside the container-structured hotel.
The final exterior shots showcase the completed structure of Pak Dream Hotel & Restaurant, seamlessly blending the container-based design with the natural surroundings. The clean lines, uniform paint finish, and thoughtful layout reflect a modern yet practical approach to construction. Positioned near Naran’s scenic landscape, the hotel stands as a testament to smart design and efficient use of materials, offering durability, visual appeal, and an inviting presence for visitors.
